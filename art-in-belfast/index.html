Art in Belfast

Places to look, make and discover.

Belfast has many ways into art: public collections, small galleries, working studios, street art, workshops and spaces made by communities. This independent selection is shared from the art at brooke.ltd to help you find others in the space.

This is an unaffiliated list. Inclusion does not imply partnership, sponsorship, representation or endorsement in either direction. It is a starting point rather than a complete directory or a ranking. Follow each organisation’s own link for current exhibitions, opening arrangements, access information, prices and booking.
